Dobby Agent Lab Uploader

Dobby Agent Lab Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the Dobby Agent Lab YouTube channel to upload videos to their own channel via the YouTube Data API.

This is not a public service. The application is operated solely by the channel owner and only accesses the YouTube account that explicitly authorizes it.

What it does

  • Uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Reads the upload and processing status of those videos.
