Privacy Policy

Dobby Agent Lab Uploader
Effective date: 2026-10-06

This Privacy Policy describes how Dobby Agent Lab Uploader (“the App”) accesses, uses, and protects information. The App is a private tool used by the owner of the Dobby Agent Lab YouTube channel to upload videos to their own channel. It is not offered as a public service.

1. Information the App accesses

The App only accesses the YouTube account that explicitly authorizes it through Google OAuth. It requests the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — used solely to upload videos to the authorizing owner's own YouTube channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — used solely to read the upload and processing status of videos on the owner's own channel.

The App does not access any other Google account data, and does not access any data belonging to other YouTube users.

2. How information is used

Data obtained through the YouTube Data API is used only to provide the App's single function: uploading videos to the owner's channel and confirming their upload status. It is not used for any other purpose, including advertising, profiling, or analytics.

3. Data collection, storage, and sharing

  • The App does not collect, store, sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's private machine and are never transmitted to any server other than Google's.
  • The App has no backend database and does not retain YouTube user data beyond what is needed to complete an upload and check its status.
  • This website uses no cookies, analytics, tracking, or third-party scripts.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Dobby Agent Lab Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Limited Use: Data obtained from Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the App's user-facing feature (uploading videos and reading their upload status for the owner's own channel). It is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide that feature, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets with notice to users. It is not used for serving advertisements, and humans do not read this data except with the owner's affirmative consent, for security purposes, to comply with applicable law, or when aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

The App also uses YouTube API Services; by using the App you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once access is revoked, the App can no longer access your YouTube account, and any stored tokens become invalid.

6. Data deletion

Because the App does not store personal data on any server, there is nothing to delete beyond the OAuth tokens on the owner's private machine, which are invalidated upon revocation. For any deletion request, contact the email below.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
